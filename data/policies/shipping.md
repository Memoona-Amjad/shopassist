# Shipping Policy

Lawn & Loom ships to every city in Pakistan through our courier partners (TCS, Leopards and M&P).

## Delivery times
- **Karachi, Lahore, Islamabad and Rawalpindi:** 2–3 working days after dispatch.
- **Other major cities** (Faisalabad, Multan, Peshawar, Quetta, Hyderabad, Sialkot, Gujranwala): 3–5 working days.
- **Smaller towns and remote areas:** 5–7 working days.
- Orders are dispatched within **1–2 working days** of being placed. During sale events dispatch can take up to 4 working days.
- Sundays and public holidays are not working days.

## Shipping charges
- Flat **Rs 250** shipping on orders below Rs 5,000.
- **Free shipping** on orders of Rs 5,000 or more.

## Cash on Delivery (COD)
- COD is available in all cities for orders up to **Rs 25,000**.
- Orders above Rs 25,000 must be paid by card or bank transfer.
- Please keep the exact amount ready. Riders cannot accept cards.
- You may not open the parcel before paying the rider, but you can refuse an obviously damaged parcel.

## Tracking your order
- When your order ships you get an SMS with the courier name and tracking number.
- You can also ask our chat assistant for your order status. You will need your order number and the last 4 digits of the phone number used on the order.

## International shipping
- We do not ship outside Pakistan at the moment.
