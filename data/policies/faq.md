# Frequently Asked Questions

## Is Lawn & Loom a real store?
Lawn & Loom is a fictional demo store used to show the ShopAssist support agent. No real orders are placed.

## Are your fabrics pure lawn?
Our lawn collection is 100% cotton lawn, 80/80 count. Our formal pieces use chiffon, organza and silk blends; the exact fabric is listed on each product.

## Do you restock sold-out items?
Popular designs are sometimes restocked within 4–6 weeks. Unstitched designs from a seasonal collection are usually not restocked.

## Can I change my order after placing it?
You can change the size, address or phone number while the order is still **processing**. Once it has shipped we cannot change it, but you can return or exchange it after delivery.

## Do you offer stitching services?
Not at the moment. All our stitched items come in standard sizes (see the size guide).

## How do I care for lawn?
Hand wash or machine wash on a gentle cycle in cold water, with similar colours. Dry in the shade and iron on medium heat. Do not bleach.

## Do you have a physical shop?
No. Lawn & Loom is online only.

## Which payment methods do you accept?
Cash on Delivery, debit and credit cards (Visa and Mastercard), and bank transfer.

## Do you have gift cards?
Not yet.
