# Size Guide

All measurements are in **inches** and refer to the garment, not the body. If you are between two sizes, we suggest the larger size.

## Unstitched suits
- Unstitched 3-piece lawn suits include: shirt fabric (about 3 metres), dupatta (2.5 metres) and trouser fabric (2.5 metres).
- Unstitched fabric is "one size" and suits most body types up to size XL when stitched.

## Fit notes
- Our lawn kurtas have a **regular fit**. Formal pieces have a **slim fit**, so consider sizing up.
- Fabric may shrink up to 2% after the first wash. Always wash cold.
