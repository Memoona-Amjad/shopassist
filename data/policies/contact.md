# Contact Us

## Customer support hours
- Monday to Saturday, **10 am to 7 pm** (Pakistan time).
- We are closed on Sundays and public holidays.

## How to reach us
- **Chat:** our assistant on the website is available 24/7 and can pass your chat to a human agent.
- **Email:** support@lawnandloom.example. We reply within 1 working day.
- **WhatsApp:** 0300-0000000 (demo number, messages only).

## Talking to a human
If the chat assistant cannot solve your problem, or if you would simply prefer a person, ask the assistant for a human. A team member will contact you within **4 working hours** during support hours.

## Complaints
We take every complaint seriously. Complaints are reviewed by a team lead and answered within 2 working days.
