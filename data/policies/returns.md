# Returns and Exchanges Policy

We want you to love what you ordered. If something is not right, you can return it.

## Return window
- You can request a return within **14 days of delivery**.
- Orders that have not been delivered yet cannot be returned. If you want to cancel an order that is still processing, please contact our team.

## Conditions
- Items must be **unworn, unwashed and unaltered**, with all original tags attached.
- Items must be in the original packaging.
- **Not returnable:** stitched-to-measure (custom tailored) orders, items bought in the final clearance sale, and unstitched fabric that has been cut.
- Dupattas and accessories can be returned only if still sealed.

## How to start a return
1. Ask our chat assistant to start a return, or contact our support team.
2. Share your order number, the last 4 digits of your phone number and the reason for the return.
3. Our courier collects the parcel from your address within 3–5 working days. Return pickup is **free**.

## Refunds
- Once the item reaches our warehouse and passes a quality check, your refund is processed within **5–7 working days**.
- COD orders are refunded by bank transfer (IBFT) or as store credit, your choice.
- Card payments are refunded to the same card.
- Original shipping charges (Rs 250) are not refunded.

## Exchanges
- Size exchanges are free, subject to stock. Request an exchange the same way as a return and mention the size you want.

## Damaged or wrong items
- If you received a damaged or wrong item, tell us within **48 hours of delivery** with a photo. We will replace it or refund it in full, including shipping.
